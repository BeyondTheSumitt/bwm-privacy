Privacy Policy

Better Work Momentum  ·  Last updated: July 12, 2026

Better Work Momentum ("we," "our," or "the extension") is a Chrome extension that helps job seekers draft LinkedIn comments using AI. This policy explains what data we handle, how we handle it, and your rights.

1. What Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Access token. A token you paste into the extension to activate your account. It is stored locally in your browser and is never transmitted except to verify your identity with our backend.
  • LinkedIn post text. When you click "Use this post," the visible text of that post is sent to our backend to generate comment drafts. We do not store the raw post text. A non-reversible fingerprint of the post is recorded in our usage log for analytics only.
  • Resume and LinkedIn profile PDFs (optional). If you choose to upload a PDF, it is transmitted to our backend, parsed to extract structured professional information, and then permanently deleted from our servers. The raw PDF and raw extracted text are never stored after the extraction step completes.
  • Structured profile data. The structured information extracted from your PDF (job titles, employers, skills, highlights) is stored locally in your browser. A non-reversible fingerprint of this profile is recorded in our usage log. The raw profile content is never stored on our servers.
  • User preferences. Tone, writing style, target role, target industry, and experience snippets are stored locally in your browser. These are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Usage data. Each request generates a usage record containing: a non-reversible identifier for your account, a non-reversible identifier for the post, the service tier used, response time, and a timestamp. No raw text of any kind is included in usage records.
  • LinkedIn analytics you choose to capture (optional). If you choose to track your momentum, the extension reads the performance figures LinkedIn already shows you — such as profile views and search appearances — and keeps a weekly summary so you can see your progress over time. This happens only when you start a capture, and only on your own LinkedIn analytics pages.
  • Your target list. When you add a person or company to your target list, the extension saves the basic professional details you are already viewing — such as a name, headline, and profile link — so you can organize and track your outreach. This is used only to power your target list and your personalized guidance.
  • Imported LinkedIn data (optional). You can choose to import a copy of your own LinkedIn data export to personalize your profile. It is handled the same way as an uploaded PDF: used to build your structured profile, then not retained in raw form.
  • Personalized reports. The extension can generate a personalized targeting report from the information you have already provided. Reports are built from your existing profile and preferences — no new personal data is collected to create them.

2. How We Store Data

  • Local browser storage. Your access token, profile data, and preferences are stored on your device only. We cannot access this data remotely.
  • Server-side storage. Our backend stores usage records and, for the optional features you turn on (your target list, captured analytics summaries, and reports), the information described above — so those features keep working over time. Sensitive identifiers are converted to non-reversible fingerprints, and we do not store the raw text of the posts you comment on or the raw files you upload.
  • Usage data retention. Usage records are deleted on a rolling 90-day cycle.

3. PDF Handling

When you upload a resume or LinkedIn profile PDF:

  1. The file is transmitted securely to our backend servers.
  2. The file is held temporarily in secure cloud storage for processing only.
  3. Text is extracted from the PDF and processed by an AI model to produce a structured profile.
  4. The PDF file is permanently deleted from our servers immediately after extraction completes — whether extraction succeeds or fails.
  5. The raw extracted text is discarded after the structured profile is returned.

We do not retain, sell, or share your resume or LinkedIn PDF content.

4. AI Processing

Comment drafts and profile extraction are processed using a cloud-hosted AI service operating in the United States. Post text and extracted profile content are transmitted to this service for processing. By default, our AI provider does not use submitted data to train or improve its models.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Data is shared only with our cloud infrastructure provider, which handles backend computation, storage, and AI processing as described above.

6. Your Rights

  • Access and deletion. Because all identifying data is converted to non-reversible fingerprints before server-side storage, we cannot reconstruct or retrieve your personal information from our servers. Your locally stored data (token, profile, preferences) can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser's extension storage.
  • Opting out. You may use the extension without uploading a PDF. Profile-based personalization is entirely optional.

7. Chrome Extension Permissions

Better Work Momentum requests only the permissions necessary to function. Here is what each one does:

  • Read the current LinkedIn post. When you use the extension on a LinkedIn post, it reads the visible text of that post to generate comment drafts. It does not read any other content on the page.
  • Save your settings locally. Your access token, profile data, and preferences are saved directly on your device. We cannot access this data remotely.
  • Add a right-click option. A "Use this post" option is added to your right-click menu when you are on LinkedIn.
  • Display the side panel. The BWM panel opens alongside LinkedIn in your browser window.
  • Detect LinkedIn pages. The extension checks whether you are on LinkedIn so it knows when to activate. It does not monitor or record your activity on any other site.
  • Open your LinkedIn analytics pages during a capture. When you start an analytics capture, the extension briefly opens your own LinkedIn analytics pages to read the figures LinkedIn shows you, then returns you to where you were. This happens only when you ask for a capture.
  • LinkedIn only. Better Work Momentum operates exclusively on linkedin.com. It has no access to any other website you visit.

A note on Chrome's "Read your browsing history" warning. When you install Better Work Momentum, Chrome may display a warning that says "Read your browsing history." This is Chrome's generic label for extensions that need to detect which site you are on — it does not mean BWM reads, records, stores, or transmits your browsing history. This capability is used solely to detect when you are viewing a LinkedIn page so the extension can activate. BWM has no visibility into your activity on any other site.

8. Children's Privacy

Better Work Momentum is intended for adults engaged in professional job seeking. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13.
