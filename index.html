Better Work Momentum — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Better Work Momentum is a Chrome extension that helps job seekers write better LinkedIn comments. This policy explains, in plain English, what information the extension handles, where that information goes, how long it stays there, and what you can do about it.

We have tried to keep this short and specific. If anything here is unclear, email us at bwm.support@gmail.com and we will answer.

The short version. We do not sell your data. We do not run ads. We do not post anything to LinkedIn on your behalf. Your resume file is deleted from our servers as soon as we have read it. Your LinkedIn analytics numbers never leave your browser. The things we do keep on our servers are listed below, item by item.

Who we are

Better Work Momentum is operated by Beyond The Sumitt. You can reach us at bwm.support@gmail.com.

What the extension collects

It is easiest to think about this in two buckets: things that stay on your computer, and things that reach our servers.

Stays on your computer only

  • Your access token. The token your coach gives you is stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. It is sent to our servers only as an authorization header when the extension makes a request, and we store only a non-reversible hash of it, never the token itself.
  • Your extracted profile. The skills, titles, industries, and experience we pull out of your resume are written to local storage in your browser and used to make your comment drafts sound like you.
  • Your LinkedIn analytics snapshots. When you click "Capture analytics," the extension reads your own profile view count and search appearance count from your own LinkedIn analytics pages and saves them locally so the dashboard can chart your trend. These numbers are never uploaded. Our servers record only a yes/no flag that a capture happened, so the onboarding checklist can mark that step complete.
  • Your interface state. Which sections you have unlocked, which tips you have dismissed, and similar preferences.

Every record above is tied to a non-reversible identifier derived from your access token, not to your name or email address. We do not ask you for your name, your email address, or your LinkedIn login, and the extension never sees your LinkedIn password.

About the people you add to My Targets

When you add someone to your target list, we save four things from their public LinkedIn profile — name, title, company, and profile URL — plus the persona category you picked. That is how your list stays available and how the dashboard groups it.

We never contact these people, and we never sell or share their information. We do not collect anything private about them: no email, no phone number, no messages, nothing behind a login.

Remove someone from your list and their record goes with them.

What the extension does not do

  • It never posts, likes, comments, connects, follows, or messages on LinkedIn for you. Every draft is copied by you and posted by you.
  • It never reads any website other than LinkedIn.com. It is completely inert everywhere else.
  • It never reads your browsing history.
  • It never sells or rents data to anyone, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers in the extension.
  • It never uses your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

About that "Read your browsing history" warning

When you install Better Work Momentum, Chrome may warn you that the extension can "read your browsing history." That wording is Chrome's, not ours, and it is the same message shown for any extension that uses the tabs permission.

Here is what we actually use it for. The extension needs to know when the tab you are on is a LinkedIn profile page, so it can enable the "Add to My Targets" button for that specific profile. It also uses it during the "Capture analytics" action, where it sends your existing LinkedIn tab to your own analytics pages, reads your two numbers, and returns the tab to where it was.

The extension only has access to LinkedIn.com pages — that is the only host permission it requests — so there is no browsing history for it to read. It cannot see your other tabs' contents, and it does not record, store, or transmit any list of pages you have visited.

Who else sees your data

We use one service provider, and nothing else: Microsoft Azure. Azure hosts our backend and stores the data listed in the table above, and Azure OpenAI Service generates your comment drafts from the post text and the relevant parts of your professional background. Both run in Microsoft data centres in the United States.

Microsoft does not use content submitted to Azure OpenAI Service to train or improve any models, including its own or OpenAI's. Your post text and profile details are used to produce your drafts and nothing else.

As part of Azure OpenAI Service, Microsoft stores the text sent for generation and the drafts returned for up to 30 days, so that automated systems can screen for abuse of the service. Content is reviewed by a Microsoft employee only if those automated systems flag it. Microsoft deletes it after 30 days. This is Microsoft's process, not ours — we do not keep a copy of the post text on our own servers.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to, or to protect the safety and rights of our users. If Beyond The Sumitt is ever acquired, your data may transfer as part of that transaction, and we will say so here before it happens.

If your access token came from a coach, your coach can see that a token they issued is active and how much aggregate activity it has. Your coach does not see the text of your comments or the contents of your resume.

Security

All traffic between the extension and our servers runs over HTTPS. Access tokens are stored as one-way hashes, so a copy of our database does not yield a working token. Secrets are held in Azure Key Vault and our services authenticate to each other using managed identities rather than shared passwords. Access to production data is limited to the people who operate the service.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify affected users.

Your choices

  • See what we have. Email us and we will send you the records associated with your token.
  • Delete it. Email us and we will delete your server-side data, including your target list. To clear the local side, remove the extension from Chrome — that erases everything it stored on your device.
  • Stop uploading anything new. Uninstalling the extension ends all data collection immediately.

Requests go to bwm.support@gmail.com and we aim to respond within 30 days.

Children

Better Work Momentum is intended for adult job seekers. It is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has used the extension, contact us and we will delete the records.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or what we do with it, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the extension itself.
